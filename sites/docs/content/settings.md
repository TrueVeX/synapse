Open **Settings** with the **gear icon** at the top of the sidebar, or from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar. On phones, Settings opens full-screen, with tabs you can scroll across.

Settings has four tabs: **General**, **Personalization**, **Data controls** and **Account**. Most changes save instantly. In Personalization, the two text boxes save when you select **Save**.

## General

### Theme

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System follows your phone's or computer's setting and switches automatically when it changes. The theme is saved **on each device**, so you can use dark on your phone and light on your laptop.

### Response language

Choose the language SYNAPSE answers in:

| Option | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Auto (match my message)** | Answers in the language you write in. This is the default |
| A specific language | Always answers in that language, unless you ask for another one in your message |

Available languages: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese.

### Default research mode

The mode selected when you start a new chat: **Web research**, **Academic** or **Deep research** (Pro and Max only). See [Research modes](/research-modes).

### Show thinking

Turn off to hide the thinking panel above answers. The model may still think; you just won't see its reasoning.

## Personalization

Personalization lets SYNAPSE tailor answers to you, like custom instructions in other assistants.

### Use personalization

The master switch. When off, SYNAPSE ignores the two text boxes below, but your answer language and response style still apply.

### Response style

| Style | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Balanced answers |
| **Concise** | Short and direct: the answer first, then only the most important details |
| **Detailed** | Thorough, well-structured answers with background, nuances and examples |
| **Formal** | A professional tone suited to reports and academic writing |
| **Friendly** | A warm, encouraging tone that explains things simply |

### What should SYNAPSE know about you?

Up to 1,500 characters about you, for example your field of study, job, location or interests.

```text
I'm a final-year pharmacy student in Hyderabad. I'm preparing for GPAT and
I'm interested in clinical research.
```

### How should SYNAPSE respond?

Up to 1,500 characters about how you like answers.

```text
Use simple language and short paragraphs. Give one real-world example.
End with a two-line summary.
```

SYNAPSE treats both boxes as **preferences**: they shape tone, format and focus, but they never override accuracy, safety, or the facts about who built SYNAPSE.

> [!TIP]
> Your name is always shared with SYNAPSE so it can address you naturally. Change it in **Account → Full name**.

## Data controls

### Export chats

Downloads a JSON file with your account details, settings and every chat, including messages, sources and the names of attached files. Use it as a backup or to move your research elsewhere.

### Delete all chats

Permanently deletes every chat in your history after you confirm. Your account, plan, credits and settings are kept. This can't be undone.

## Account

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Change the name SYNAPSE uses for you. Select **Save** |
| **Email** | The email you sign in with. It can't be changed yet |
| **Plan** | Your plan, credits left and auto-renew status, with a link to **Upgrade** or **Manage plan** |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out on this device |
| **Delete account** | Permanently deletes your account. See below |

### Deleting your account

1. Select **Delete account**.
2. Type your email address to confirm, then select **Delete account**.

SYNAPSE then **cancels any auto-pay** with Razorpay first, and deletes your chats, settings, credit records and subscription. Payment records are kept for accounting, without any link to you. You're signed out and can't undo this.

## Where settings are stored

Everything except the theme is saved to your account, so it follows you to every browser and to the Windows and Android apps. The theme, and whether the sidebar is collapsed, are saved in each browser or app.
