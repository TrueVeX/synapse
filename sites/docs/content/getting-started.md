This guide walks you through your first session with SYNAPSE. You need a web browser, or the Windows or Android app, and an email address or Google account.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open [synapse-humanform.vercel.app](https://synapse-humanform.vercel.app/signup).
2. Choose how to sign up:
   - **Continue with Google**: pick your Google account and allow access. SYNAPSE receives your name and email address from Google, and nothing else.
   - **Email**: enter your full name, email address and a password of **at least 8 characters**, then select **Create account**.
3. You land on the SYNAPSE home screen, greeted by name, with **110 free credits** ready for today.

> [!NOTE]
> Google sign-in works on the website. Inside the Windows and Android apps the Google button is hidden, because Google doesn't allow its sign-in inside app windows. If you created your account with Google, see [Accounts and sign-in](/accounts) for how to use the apps.

## 2. Ask your first question

Type a question in the box in the middle of the screen, for example:

```text
What does current research say about the health effects of intermittent fasting?
```

Press **Enter** to send. On a computer, **Shift + Enter** adds a new line; on phones, Enter adds a new line and you tap the send button instead.

Not sure what to ask? Select one of the suggestion chips under the box (**Research**, **Compare**, **Learn**, **Analyze a file**) to see example prompts, then pick one to fill the box.

## 3. Watch the answer arrive

Each answer happens in stages you can follow:

1. **Searched the web**: SYNAPSE writes a search based on your question and the chat so far.
2. **Sources**: the pages it found appear in a compact list you can expand.
3. **Thinking**: a shimmering "thinking" label appears while the model works. Some models also share their reasoning, which you can open in a **thinking panel**.
4. **The answer** streams in word by word, with numbered citation chips.

While an answer is being written, the send button becomes a **Stop** button. Stopping keeps whatever was written so far. If you stop before any words appear, the credits for that answer are returned.

## 4. Ask follow-up questions

Type your next question in the box at the bottom of the chat. SYNAPSE uses the recent messages in the chat (up to the last 12) as context. Very short follow-ups, under eight words, are combined with your previous question when SYNAPSE searches, so they still find relevant pages.

## 5. Find your chats again

Every conversation is saved automatically and listed under **Recents** in the sidebar, newest first. The first question becomes the chat's name (up to 60 characters).

- **Open** a chat by selecting it. Each chat has its own address, like `/chat/…`, so the browser's back and forward buttons work and you can bookmark a chat. Chat links only open for your own account.
- **Rename** or **delete** a chat with the **…** menu next to its name.
- **Start a new chat** with **New chat** at the top of the sidebar, or the pencil icon on phones.
- **Collapse the sidebar** on a computer with the panel icon; SYNAPSE remembers your choice.

## 6. Pick a research mode

The menu next to the send button sets the research mode for your next question:

| Mode | Best for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Web research** | Everyday questions, comparisons, explanations | 1 credit |
| **Academic** | Formal, evidence-focused answers that define terms | 1 credit |
| **Deep research** | Detailed investigations with an advanced search (Pro and Max) | 3 credits on Pro, 2 on Max |

The clock-shaped **extended thinking** button also switches a question to Deep research. Read more in [Research modes](/research-modes).

## 7. Attach files

Select **+** next to the message box, or drag files onto it, to attach documents and images. Pasting more than 300 characters of text turns it into a "pasted" attachment so your message stays readable. See [Files and images](/files-and-images) for formats and limits.

## 8. Keep an eye on your credits

Your plan and remaining credits are shown at the bottom of the sidebar. When you're running low, a notice appears above the message box with the time your credits reset. Free credits refresh every day at midnight India time (IST). See [Plans and credits](/plans-and-credits).

## The SYNAPSE screen

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Sidebar** (left) | New chat, your recent chats, your plan and credits, and your account menu |
| **Settings** (gear icon) | Theme, language, response style, personalization, data controls and account |
| **Header** | The current chat's name; on phones, the menu and new-chat buttons |
| **Conversation** | Your questions on the right, SYNAPSE's answers on the left |
| **Message box** | Type, attach files, choose a mode, send or stop |
| **Jump to latest** | An arrow button that appears when you scroll up during a long answer |
