Every SYNAPSE account starts on **Free**. Answers use **credits**; paid plans give you more credits, Deep research and more files per message. All prices are in Indian rupees (INR).

## The plans

| | **Free** | **Pro** | **Max** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price, monthly billing** | ₹0 | ₹499 / month | ₹1,299 / month |
| **Price, yearly billing** | ₹0 | ₹4,788 / year (₹399 / month) | ₹12,468 / year (₹1,039 / month) |
| **Credits** | 110 every day | 600 every month | 1,500 every month |
| **Web research and Academic** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Deep research and extended thinking** | – | ✓ (3 credits) | ✓ (2 credits) |
| **Files per message** | 1 | 5 | 10 |
| **Chat history, settings, apps** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Yearly billing saves about 20% compared with paying monthly. Compare and buy plans on the [pricing page](https://synapse-humanform.vercel.app/pricing).

## How credits work

Each answer costs credits, taken when you send the question:

| Answer | Free | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web research | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Academic | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Deep research / extended thinking | not available | 3 | 2 |
| Retry | Same as asking again | Same as asking again | Same as asking again |

Attachments don't cost extra credits, but the number of files per message depends on your plan.

### When credits come back

Credits are returned automatically when:

- an answer **fails** before writing anything (for example, the AI service is busy), or
- you **stop** an answer before any words appear.

If an answer has started writing when you stop it, the credits are used.

## When credits reset

| Plan | Reset |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Every day at **midnight India time (IST)**. You get 110 fresh credits each day. |
| **Pro and Max** | Every **30 days**, counted from the day your subscription started. This is the same on yearly billing: a yearly plan refreshes your monthly allowance every 30 days for the whole year. |

Unused credits don't roll over to the next day or month.

> [!NOTE]
> When you renew the same plan, your 30-day credit cycle keeps its original start date. When you **change** plan (for example Pro → Max), a new cycle starts on the day the new plan begins.

## Seeing your credits

- **Sidebar**: your plan name and a meter showing credits left and when they reset.
- **Low-credit notice**: when you have about 20% left (or 2 credits, whichever is more), a notice appears above the message box with an **Upgrade** link.
- **Pricing page**: your plan, credits, renewal date and payment history.
- **Settings → Account → Plan**: a one-line summary with a link to manage your plan.

## Running out

When you don't have enough credits for an answer, SYNAPSE doesn't send the question. Instead the answer area explains how many credits you used, when they reset, and offers a **View plans** button. Nothing is charged.

You'll also see an explanation, and no credits are used, when:

- you choose **Deep research** on Free (it's part of Pro and Max), or
- you attach **more files** than your plan allows.

## Choosing a plan

| You… | Choose |
|---|---|
| ask a few questions a day and don't need Deep research | **Free** |
| research most days, want Deep research, or attach several files at once | **Pro** |
| run many Deep research sessions or work with up to 10 files at once | **Max**: Deep research costs only 2 credits |

Upgrade, switch or cancel any time; see [Billing and auto-pay](/billing).
