---
title: Live mode
description: Talk with SYNAPSE out loud, and show it your camera or your screen, in a real-time conversation.
---

Live mode turns SYNAPSE into a voice assistant. You talk, SYNAPSE answers out loud, and you can show it what's in front of you through your camera or your screen. It's like a phone call with a research assistant who can see what you see.

## Start Live

1. Open SYNAPSE and leave the message box empty.
2. Select the **Live** button (the sound-wave icon where the send button usually is).
3. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
4. Start talking. When you pause, SYNAPSE answers.

A glowing orb shows what's happening:

- **Listening**: the orb breathes with your voice.
- **Thinking**: the orb pulses while SYNAPSE works out the answer, and checks the web if your question needs fresh facts ("What's the weather in Hyderabad today?").
- **Speaking**: rings ripple out while SYNAPSE talks. The words also appear on screen as captions.

When it finishes speaking, it listens again automatically, so you can keep the conversation going hands-free.

## Interrupt

Tap the orb while SYNAPSE is speaking or thinking. It stops at once and listens to you.

## Show your camera or screen

- **Camera**: select the camera button. SYNAPSE sees what your camera sees each time you speak ("What plant is this?", "Read this label for me"). On phones, use **Switch camera** to change between the front and back cameras.
- **Screen**: select the screen button and choose a window, tab or your whole screen ("What does this error mean?", "Summarise this chart"). Screen sharing works in desktop browsers.

A label on the preview reminds you that SYNAPSE can see it. Turn the camera or screen off at any time with the same button. SYNAPSE only looks at a single snapshot when you finish speaking, not a continuous video.

## Type instead

Select the **keyboard** button to type your turn instead of speaking. SYNAPSE still answers out loud. Typing is used automatically where voice recognition isn't available.

## Mute and end

- **Mute** turns the microphone off until you select it again.
- **End** closes Live mode and opens the conversation as a normal chat titled **Live: ...**, so you can read the whole transcript, copy answers, or continue by typing.

## The voice

SYNAPSE speaks with a natural voice from **ElevenLabs** when it's set up on the server. Otherwise it uses the best voice installed on your device (for example the Microsoft "Natural" voices on Windows or Google voices in Chrome). The top-right corner of Live mode shows which one is in use.

## Where it works

| | Voice | Camera | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Chrome on Android | ✓ | ✓ | (not supported by Android browsers) |
| Safari on iPhone and Mac | ✓ | ✓ | Mac only |
| Firefox | Type instead | ✓ | ✓ |
| SYNAPSE Windows and Android apps | Type instead | ✓ | Windows app: coming soon |

Voice recognition uses your browser's built-in speech service, which is why it works best in Chrome and Edge.

## Credits and privacy

- Each Live turn uses the same credits as a Web research answer (1 credit). If a turn fails, the credit comes back.
- Your voice is turned into text by your browser; SYNAPSE receives the text, not a recording.
- A camera or screen snapshot is sent only with the turn you just spoke, is used to answer it, and isn't stored. The chat shows *Camera view* or *Shared screen* next to the question so you know it was included.
