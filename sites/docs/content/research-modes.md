title: Research modes
description: Web research, Academic and Deep research, what each one does, what it costs and when to use it.

SYNAPSE has three research modes. Each one changes how SYNAPSE searches and how it writes the answer. Choose a mode from the menu next to the send button before you ask.

## Overview

| | Web research | Academic | Deep research |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search depth** | Basic | Basic | Advanced |
| **Sources read** | Up to 6 | Up to 6 | Up to 6 |
| **Writing style** | Clear and direct | Formal; defines terms; separates evidence from interpretation | Detailed and structured; compares evidence; explains uncertainty |
| **Available on** | All plans | All plans | Pro and Max |
| **Credits** | 1 | 1 | 3 on Pro, 2 on Max |

## Web research

The default mode, and the right choice for most questions. SYNAPSE runs a focused web search, reads the most relevant results and writes a clear answer with citations.

Use it for:

- Quick factual questions ("When does the monsoon usually reach Kerala?")
- Comparisons ("Compare the iPhone and Pixel cameras")
- Explanations ("How does a heat pump work?")
- Current events, where fresh sources matter

## Academic

Academic mode uses the same search but asks the model to write in a clear academic style: it defines important terms and keeps **evidence** (what sources show) separate from **interpretation** (what that might mean).

Use it for:

- Studying and revision
- Literature-style overviews of a topic
- Writing that needs careful, neutral wording

> [!TIP]
> Academic mode doesn't search academic databases specifically. It searches the open web and writes in an academic style. For peer-reviewed sources, ask for them explicitly, for example "Cite peer-reviewed studies where possible".

## Deep research

Deep research uses Tavily's **advanced** search depth, which looks harder for relevant content, and asks the model for a detailed, structured investigation that compares evidence and is honest about uncertainty.

Use it for:

- Questions with several sides or conflicting evidence
- Market, product or policy research
- Anything you'd otherwise spend an hour searching for yourself

Deep research is part of **Pro** and **Max**. On Free, the mode shows a **Pro** badge; selecting it explains how to upgrade. It costs **3 credits** on Pro and **2 credits** on Max.

### Extended thinking

The clock-shaped button in the message box turns on **extended thinking**. A question sent with extended thinking switches to Deep research, so it has the same availability and cost.

## Choosing a default mode

SYNAPSE starts every new chat in **Web research**. To start in a different mode, go to **Settings → General → Default research mode**. On Free, Deep research can't be the default; if you set it on Pro and later return to Free, new chats start in Web research.

## How follow-ups are searched

Within a chat, SYNAPSE keeps the context of earlier messages. When a follow-up is very short (fewer than eight words), SYNAPSE combines it with your previous question before searching, so "and in Europe?" after a question about electric car sales searches for European electric car sales.

## Credit refunds

If an answer fails before writing anything, or you stop it before any words appear, its credits are returned automatically. Retrying an answer costs the same as asking again. See [Plans and credits](/plans-and-credits).
