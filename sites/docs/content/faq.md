## About SYNAPSE

### What is SYNAPSE?

An AI research assistant. It searches the live web, reads the most relevant pages and any files you attach, and writes an answer with numbered citations you can check. See the [introduction](/).

### Who made SYNAPSE?

SYNAPSE was created by **Revoori Ashwath Reddy** at **HumanForm Studios**. Ask SYNAPSE "Who built you?" and it will tell you the same.

### Which AI model does SYNAPSE use?

By default, **NVIDIA Nemotron**, accessed through **OpenRouter**. OpenRouter lets SYNAPSE switch models without changing the app, so the model may change as better options become available.

### Is SYNAPSE free?

Yes. Free gives you **110 credits every day**, enough for 110 Web research or Academic answers, with no card needed. Pro and Max add Deep research, more files per message and a monthly allowance. See [Plans and credits](/plans-and-credits).

### Is SYNAPSE affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or Google?

No. SYNAPSE is an independent product by HumanForm Studios. Its colour theme is based on a community theme from 21st.dev, and it uses services from several companies, listed in [Credits](/credits), but it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

## Answers

### Can I trust SYNAPSE's answers?

Treat them like a well-researched first draft. SYNAPSE cites its sources so you can check them, and it's instructed never to invent citations, but AI can still misread or overstate a source. Always check important facts against the cited pages.

### Why did an answer have no citations?

Either web search found nothing relevant, web search was temporarily unavailable (the sources block will say so), or the answer is general reasoning that doesn't need a source. Try rephrasing the question with more specific terms.

### Does SYNAPSE remember my previous chats?

Each chat remembers its own recent messages (up to the last 12) as context. Separate chats don't share memory, but your **personalization** settings apply to every chat.

### Can SYNAPSE answer in my language?

Yes: 17 languages, including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali. By default it matches the language you write in; choose a fixed language in **Settings → General → Response language**.

### What's the difference between Academic and Deep research?

Academic changes the **writing style** (formal, defines terms, separates evidence from interpretation) using a normal search. Deep research uses an **advanced search** and writes a longer, structured investigation. See [Research modes](/research-modes).

### Can SYNAPSE read PDFs and images?

Yes: PDFs with selectable text, Word documents, text, Markdown, CSV and JSON files, and PNG, JPG, WEBP and GIF images. Scanned PDFs without text can't be read as documents. See [Files and images](/files-and-images).

## Plans and payments

### When do my credits reset?

Free: every day at midnight India time. Pro and Max: every 30 days from your subscription date, including on yearly plans.

### Do unused credits roll over?

No. Each day (Free) or 30-day cycle (Pro and Max) starts fresh.

### Do I lose credits if an answer fails?

No. If an answer fails before writing anything, or you stop it before any words appear, the credits are returned automatically.

### Which payment methods can I use?

Through Razorpay: **UPI AutoPay** (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and bank UPI apps) and **cards** with auto-debit. Available options depend on your bank and on Razorpay.

### Will I be charged automatically?

Yes. Pro and Max renew automatically every month or year until you cancel. Cancel any time on the pricing page; you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. See [Billing and auto-pay](/billing).

### How do I get a refund?

Use [Razorpay's support page](https://razorpay.com/support/) and choose the option for customers who paid a business. Your request reaches us with the payment details.

### I paid but I'm still on Free. What now?

Wait a minute and refresh the pricing page; Razorpay's notification activates the plan even if your browser closed early. If it's still Free after 10 minutes, contact us through Razorpay's support page.

## Apps

### Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. In Safari, open SYNAPSE and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for an app-like experience.

### Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

The Windows app isn't code-signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Select **More info → Run anyway**. You can verify the file's SHA-256 fingerprint on the download page.

### Why can't I sign in with Google in the apps?

Google doesn't allow its sign-in inside app windows. Use email and password in the apps. See [Accounts and sign-in](/accounts#using-the-apps).

### Do the apps work offline?

No. SYNAPSE needs the internet to search the web and run the AI model. Without a connection, the apps show a **Try again** screen.

## Privacy

### Does SYNAPSE see my card or UPI details?

No. Payment details go straight to Razorpay. SYNAPSE only receives a signed confirmation.

### Are my files stored?

No. File contents are read for one answer and then discarded; only file names stay in your chat history.

### Can I delete my data?

Yes: delete single chats, all chats, or your whole account in Settings. See [Privacy and security](/privacy#your-controls).
