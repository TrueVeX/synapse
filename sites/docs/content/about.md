**HumanForm Studios** builds software that puts people first: tools that respect your time, your intelligence and your data.

**SYNAPSE** is HumanForm Studios' AI research assistant, created by **Revoori Ashwath Reddy**.

## Creator

**Revoori Ashwath Reddy** designed and built SYNAPSE: its research engine, its interface, its plans and payments, the Windows and Android apps, its home page and this documentation.

## What we care about

- **Honest AI.** Answers should show their sources and never invent them.
- **Calm software.** Fewer distractions, clear words, and buttons that do what they say.
- **Fair pricing.** A useful free plan, simple paid plans in rupees, and cancelling as easy as subscribing.
- **Your data is yours.** Keep only what's needed, make it easy to export and delete, and never see payment details.
