Introducing SYNAPSE

An AI research assistant that reads the web for you, and shows its work.

Try SYNAPSE free Download for Windows & Android

Update on November 10, 2026: SYNAPSE now researches on its own: it plans, writes its own searches, reads full pages and checks conflicting sources before it answers, and Web research answers start within seconds. Also new: apps for Windows and Android.

Today we're introducing SYNAPSE, a research assistant built by HumanForm Studios. Ask a question and SYNAPSE searches the live web, reads the most relevant pages and any files you attach, and writes a clear answer with numbered citations, so you can check every claim against the source it came from.

SYNAPSE is designed around one rule: never invent a source. Every answer shows the searches it ran and the pages it read. When a model shares its reasoning, you can open a thinking panel and follow along. When something can't be found, SYNAPSE says so instead of guessing.

SYNAPSE is available today, free, on the web at synapse-humanform.vercel.app, and as apps for Windows 10 and 11 and Android 7.0 and later. Pro and Max plans add more credits, Deep research and more files per message.

Research that shows its work

Most AI assistants answer from memory. SYNAPSE answers from the web as it is right now. SYNAPSE works like a research agent: it decides for itself what to look up, writes its own searches, opens the most useful pages to read them in full, and searches again when sources disagree, then builds its answer from what those pages actually say. Simple questions skip all that and get an instant answer. Citations appear as small numbered chips; tap one to open the source.

Follow-up questions keep their context: SYNAPSE remembers the recent messages in a chat and folds your earlier question into short follow-ups like “what about the cost?”, so the search still finds the right pages.

How SYNAPSE answers a question

SYNAPSE runs these steps on its own, and you can watch each one happen live: its plan, every search, every page it opens, then the answer streaming in word by word. You can stop an answer at any time, and retry it with one tap.

  1. 01

    Plan

    SYNAPSE decides what it needs to know, using your question and the context of your chat.

  2. 02

    Search & read

    It runs its own searches side by side, opens the best pages in full, and reads any files or images you attached.

  3. 03

    Check

    If sources disagree or something is missing, it goes round again; otherwise it moves on.

  4. 04

    Cite

    The answer streams in with numbered citations that link straight to each source.

Made personal

Tell SYNAPSE about yourself and how you like answers written, and it applies that to every new answer. Pick a response style (concise, detailed, formal or friendly), choose one of 17 answer languages, including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali, and switch between light and dark themes.

Your personalization follows your account to every device, including the Windows and Android apps; the theme is saved on each device. You can export all your chats, or delete them, at any time.

Bring your own files

Attach documents and images to any message, or paste long text, and SYNAPSE reads them alongside the web. It works with PDF, Word (DOCX), TXT, Markdown, CSV and JSON files and PNG, JPG, WEBP and GIF images. Free accounts attach one file per message, Pro five, and Max ten. File contents are read for that answer and not stored; only the file name is kept with your message.

Use SYNAPSE everywhere

Start on the web, then take SYNAPSE with you. The apps open the live SYNAPSE service, so they always have the newest features without updates, and your chats and plan follow you everywhere.

Private by design

  • Your payment details never reach SYNAPSE. Card and UPI details go straight to Razorpay; SYNAPSE only receives a signed confirmation.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored as text. Sign-in sessions use signed tokens that expire after seven days.
  • Attachments aren't kept. File contents are read for the answer you asked for, then discarded; only the file name stays in your chat.
  • You're in control. Export every chat as a file, delete all chats, or delete your account (which also stops any auto-pay) from Settings.
  • Locked-down database. Your data lives in a Supabase Postgres database with its public data API closed off; only SYNAPSE's server can reach it, over HTTPS.

Questions are answered by an AI model through OpenRouter and web results come from Tavily; their terms apply to what you send. Avoid sharing confidential or personal documents with free AI models.
